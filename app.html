The things people ask.

Do I have to change how I book lessons?

No. Keep using your teaching calendar. Google Calendar connects directly. Apple Calendar, Outlook and most other calendars connect with a private share link, and the app shows you where to find it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You record payments yourself, which takes a few seconds, and TutorTally does the arithmetic from there. It will never quietly assume that a bank transfer belongs to a lesson.

Can parents see each other's information?

No. Each parent's link only shows their own child's lessons and payments. They cannot see your other students, your rates or anything else.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. You keep read access to your records and can export them.

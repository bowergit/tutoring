Questions

The things people ask.

Do I have to change how I book lessons?

No. Keep using your teaching calendar. Google Calendar is the direct connection. If you use Apple Calendar, it can work by syncing that calendar into Google first.

What if I teach something other than maths?

It doesn't care what you teach. Lessons, students, money owed. Tutors of any subject use it the same way.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You log payments yourself, which takes a couple of seconds, and it does the arithmetic from there. Bank connections are regulated and would push the price well past £5.

Can parents see each other's information?

No. Each parent's link only ever shows their own child's lessons and payments. They can't see your other students, your rates, or anything else.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. You keep read access to your records and can export them. Losing your payment history because you cancelled would be a rotten way to treat someone.

Is it actually £5, or £5 and then more later?

These are the terms you agree to by using Tutortally. They are written to be read, not to be impressive. If anything here is unclear, email me and I will explain it in plain English.

1. Who you are dealing with

Tutortally is built and run by Daniel Bower, a sole trader based in England, trading as Bower Maths. You can reach me at daniel@bowermaths.co.uk, and I will give you a postal address on request.

2. What Tutortally does

It reads lessons from a calendar you connect, works out what each family owes from the lessons taught and the payments you record, and writes payment request messages for you to send yourself. It gives each parent a private page showing their own child's lessons and payment status.

It does not connect to your bank, take payments from parents on your behalf, or send anything to anyone without you pressing send. It is a record keeping tool, not an accountant, and nothing in it is accounting or tax advice.

3. Your account

Accounts are currently set up by me rather than through a signup form. One account is for one tutor. Keep your password to yourself: anything done through your account is treated as done by you. Tell me straight away if you think someone else has got into it.

You need to be 18 or over to hold an account.

4. What it costs

  • Free forever for up to 3 active students. No card needed to start.
  • Pro is £5 a month when you have 4 or more active students, billed monthly through Stripe.
  • Everything is included on Pro. There are no feature tiers and no student limit.
  • An active student means a student you are currently tracking lessons or payments for and have not archived.

I am not currently VAT registered, so no VAT is added to that £5. If that changes I will tell you before it affects what you pay.

5. Changing plan

If you are on the free plan and add a fourth active student, you need to move to Pro to keep using Tutortally fully. If you archive students and come back down to 3 active students, you can return to the free plan.

6. Cancelling

Cancel Pro whenever you want, from Billing inside the app. You keep Pro access until the end of the month you have paid for, and you will not be billed again.

I do not automatically refund part months, but if you cancel just after a payment went out and you have not really used it, email me and I will refund it. I would rather do that than have you feel stung over £5.

7. If you stop paying

Nothing is deleted. If you stop paying for Pro while you still have more than 3 active students, your account may become read only until you archive students or subscribe again. You can still open it, look at your records, and export them.

If an account sits inactive and unopened for twelve months, I will email you at least thirty days before deleting the data, so you have a chance to export it or start using it again.

8. Your students and their families

You decide what goes into Tutortally about your students and their parents, so in data protection terms you are the controller of that information and I am processing it on your behalf. Practically, that means:

  • You need a lawful basis for holding it, and families should know you keep records of lessons and payments.
  • Only put in what you actually need. It is a lesson and payment record, not a place for medical details, safeguarding notes, or anything else sensitive.
  • If a parent asks you to correct or delete their child's records, you can do that yourself in the app, and I will help if you get stuck.

How I handle that data is set out in the privacy policy.

9. Parent links

Each student gets a private link. Anyone holding that link can see that one student's lessons and payment status, without signing in. They cannot see your other students, your rates, or anything else.

Treat those links the way you would treat a shared document link. If one gets forwarded somewhere you did not intend, ask me and I will issue a fresh one, which makes the old link stop working.

10. Connecting your calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Tutortally reads events from the single calendar you choose, to find your lessons. It does not write to your calendar, and it does not read your other calendars. You can disconnect at any time from inside the app, which stops the access immediately.

11. What I cannot promise

Tutortally is run by one person alongside a tutoring business. I do not offer a guaranteed uptime, a support response time, or a promise that it will never lose anything. It is backed up, and I take it seriously, but you should not treat it as the only copy of anything you could not stand to lose.

The figures it shows are worked out from what you and your calendar put in. If a lesson never made it into the calendar, or a payment was never recorded, the totals will be wrong, and they will be wrong quietly.

Beyond that, and because Pro costs £5 a month and the free plan is free: I am not liable for lost income, lost business, missed invoicing, or money you did not chase, and my total liability to you is capped at what you have paid me in the twelve months before the problem arose. If you have only used the free plan, that cap is nil, which is another reason to keep your own copy of anything critical.

If you are using Tutortally as a consumer rather than for your tutoring business, nothing here affects your statutory rights.

I can close your account with thirty days' notice and a refund of anything you have paid for time you will not get, or immediately if it is being used in a way that is unlawful or is putting other tutors' data at risk.

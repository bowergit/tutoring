# Tutortally Brand Pack

## Positioning

Tutortally is the tiny finance desk for solo tutors.

It is for private tutors who teach alone, manage parents directly, and do not want a full CRM. They need to know what happened, what was paid, and what still needs chasing, without rebuilding their diary in another tool.

## Target Client

Solo tutors with 5-40 regular students.

They are already organised enough to use Google Calendar, WhatsApp, bank transfers, and maybe a spreadsheet, but the money trail gets fuzzy when lessons move, rates change, parents pay late, or several siblings are involved.

They are not buying "software". They are buying relief from the awkward moment of not knowing whether they are owed money.

## Promise

Tutortally turns your calendar into a clear record of lessons, payments, parent links, and polite chase messages.

Short version: tutoring admin, finally totalled.

## Personality

Clear, warm, precise, and quietly on the tutor's side.

Tutortally should feel like a small, sensible tool made by someone who has actually taught lessons and chased parents. It should never sound corporate, over-automated, or like a generic school platform.

## Voice

Use plain English. Say "you", "parents", "lessons", "paid", "owes", and "chase".

Prefer:
- "Know exactly who still owes you."
- "One tap opens WhatsApp with the message written."
- "Your calendar stays the source of truth."
- "Built for a tutor between lessons."

Avoid:
- "Leverage", "streamline", "empower", "unlock".
- Enterprise phrases like "stakeholders", "pipeline", "client lifecycle".
- Making it sound as if messages send automatically.

## Core Messages

- Your Google Calendar becomes your lesson record.
- Balances are calculated from completed lessons and payments.
- Payment requests are written for you, but sent by you.
- Parents get a private lesson page.
- Rate changes do not break old balances.
- It costs less than losing one lesson payment.

## Taglines

- Tutoring admin, finally totalled.
- The tiny finance desk for solo tutors.
- Know who owes what before it gets awkward.
- Your calendar in. Clear balances out.

## Visual Identity

Logo idea: a compact tally-mark icon beside the Tutortally wordmark. The mark should feel like counting lessons on a ledger, not like a school badge.

Logo files:
- `brand/tutortally-mark.svg`
- `brand/tutortally-mark-transparent.svg`
- `brand/tutortally-logo.svg`
- `brand/tutortally-logo-reversed.svg`
- `brand/tutortally-logo-icon.png`
- `brand/tutortally-logo-mark-transparent.png`
- `brand/tutortally-logo-preview.html`

The mark uses two upright ink tally strokes crossed by one rising teal tally stroke. It should read first as a tally mark, and second as a quiet "TT" monogram. It should work as an app icon, favicon, social avatar, and compact nav mark.

Wordmark:
- "Tutor" in ink.
- "tally" in teal.
- Tight, friendly, practical.

Palette:
- Ink: `#15211f`
- Paper: `#f7f8f4`
- Soft mint: `#ecf4f1`
- Teal: `#0f766e`
- Clear blue: `#2563eb`
- Chase coral: `#d05b39`
- Receipt gold: `#b7791f`

Typography:
- General Sans for product and marketing UI.
- Use strong weights for headings, but keep body text relaxed and readable.

UI Rules:
- Cards should feel like tidy records, not marketing tiles.
- Paid states use teal.
- Owed/chase states use coral.
- Calendar and sync states can use blue.
- Money/receipt details can use gold sparingly.
- Do not overdecorate. The product should feel fast and trustworthy.

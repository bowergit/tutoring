# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS generated by a small Node build script, deployed on Cloudflare (temporarily under tutortally.co.uk/coachbower/, later its own repo at coachbower.com root). All internal links must stay relative so the folder can move to a domain root unchanged.

## Users

Adults in Montreal who have been sidelined from activities they love by pain or injury: runners, golfers, rec hockey players, dancers, people who just want to walk the dog or carry groceries without pain. Often skeptical of trainers, often stuck in a loop of physio appointments that help short-term but don't rebuild strength. They arrive from Instagram, Google reviews or word of mouth, and are deciding whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Coach Michael Bower's website. It turns a curious, slightly skeptical visitor into an enquiry for coaching. Primary: in-person rehab and strength coaching in Montreal. Secondary: online coaching anywhere. The blog (weekly articles) builds trust and feeds the mailing list.

## Positioning

Bridges the gap between physio and personal training: rehab principles combined with performance training, so clients get out of pain and keep building strength. Academic background in Sport Science & Physiology, nearly 10 years coaching. "Without the fluff." Clients are "athletes of life."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact goes through his Google Form (no server-side form) plus email hello@coachbower.com.
- Mailing list / lead magnet: Mailchimp page "12 Essential Isometrics for Runners".
- Instagram @coachbower_.
- Premade programs are mentioned in the FAQ; whether they are on sale is unconfirmed. Do not add prices, product pages or buy buttons.
- Blog posts are text only; they have no images on the original site.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the brain logo (img/logo.png, white on transparent).
- Keep hot pink (#ff1289) as the brand colour.
- Voice: direct, warm, casual, a bit cheeky ("without the fluff", "WTF is progressive overload?"). First person, Michael speaking.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six client stories with real photos (Howard, Josee-Anne, Richard, Pam, Fady, Gino) and Instagram video links.
- Six real Google reviews (names and text as published).
- Headshot, coaching photo, hero barbell photo, eight Instagram images.
- 30 blog posts.
- No pricing, no certifications list, no press. Do not invent any of these, or any stats beyond what the stories state.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the visitor's pain and the activity they want back, not with the coach.
2. Proof is real people: stories and reviews carry the persuasion.
3. Every page offers one clear next step: get in touch.
4. Plain talk over fitness-industry hype.
